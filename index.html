Born on a station bench

Platform 9 Chai started with one kettle near a suburban station, serving commuters who only had a few minutes. We kept that rhythm: quick service, honest prices and chai that tastes the same every morning.

Today we still brew in small batches, buy tea leaves from Assam growers and pour into glass, not paper.
